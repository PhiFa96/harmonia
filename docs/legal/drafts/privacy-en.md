## Additional P3.04 working draft: Weekly leagues

When signed in, your first newly confirmed XP each week place you in a league group. Other group members see only an identifier within that group, your rank and weekly XP; your private profile name and email address are never published. Weekly results and read/delivery markers are kept for the current and the last 52 completed weeks; tier and category preference remain until account deletion. Pruning requires the configured league job. Result system notifications have a separate opt-in from learning reminders and default to off. Both require global push permission and share the daily limit. **[Obtain legal review of purpose, legal basis, visibility and retention.]**

## Theory Rush (additional working draft)

When you start a Rush run with your account, we process the mode, server-generated questions, answers, confirmation times, mistakes, points and daily allowance used. Leaderboards shown to signed-in learners contain only rank and points without your profile name, email address or account identifier. The friends view includes only mutually confirmed connections; blocked or removed connections do not count. Raw answers and their question source are removed no later than twelve months after the run started; confirmed results and XP remain until account deletion. You can export your own data and delete your account. **[Obtain legal review of purpose, lawful basis, visibility, required notices and retention. This is not a published privacy notice.]**

## Duels (additional working draft)

For a duel we process the server-issued shared questions, your answers and times, score and result. Live duels also update your duel rating and award XP. The opponent sees only your progress and score, not your profile name or email. Ghosts are clearly labelled as recordings of real verified Rush runs. Invitations use a random link or code. Invitation and ghost duels do not affect ratings or award XP. Raw duel answers and question sources are removed no later than twelve months after the game started; confirmed results and XP remain until account deletion. Open invitations are removed within 30 days after expiry. You can export your own data. **[Obtain legal review of purpose, lawful basis, visibility, protections for minors and retention. ## Friends and weekly quests (additional working draft)

When you create or accept a random one-time link or code, we process the internal account IDs, invitation and confirmed friendship. Confirmed friends see only an anonymous identifier, your stored learning streak and league tier. A weekly quest started together shows each participant the XP confirmed since it began and awards each a badge at 300 combined XP. There is no name, email or contact search, and no chat. You can remove or block a friend; this ends an active quest and excludes the relationship from the Rush friends board. Earned badges remain. Invitations are deleted 30 days after expiry, quest pairings after 52 weeks; relationships and badges remain until account deletion. You can export your own data. **[Obtain legal review of purpose, lawful basis, visibility, protections for minors and retention. This is not a published privacy notice.]**

## Daily puzzle (additional working draft)

When signed in, we process one answer per local calendar day, its correct/incorrect result, time and reported time zone for your own puzzle streak. Everyone receives the same question for a given date. The optional shared result text contains only the date, a success symbol and streak length; the app shares it only when you choose to. Answer, exact time and time zone are removed after twelve months, while the date and result remain as streak evidence until account deletion. You can export your results. **[Obtain legal review of purpose, lawful basis, time-zone handling and retention. This is not a published privacy notice.]**
